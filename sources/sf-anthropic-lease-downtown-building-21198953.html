Tech titan Anthropic in discussions to take over entire building in downtown S.F.

By Laura Waxmann, Staff WriterNov 20, 2025

For months, rumors have been swirling that artificial intelligence industry pioneer Anthropic has its sights set on an empty office building in the shadow of San Francisco’s Salesforce Park that is slated for a major makeover.

Those rumors were substantiated Thursday as sources familiar with Anthropic’s office space requirements confirmed to the Chronicle that the company is in talks for a long-term lease at 300 Howard St. — formerly known as 199 Fremont St. and previously leased to Fitbit, now part of Google, and StubHub.

Those sources said Anthropic’s new lease commitment, which is not finalized but appears to be progressing, would be for a term of 13 years. It would involve the company taking over the entire 420,000-square-foot building, which sits not far from where Anthropic already leases office space at 500 and 505 Howard St.

It’s unclear whether the company, which raised a staggering $13 billion in September, is currently evaluating any other buildings. A spokesperson for Anthropic did not comment on the pending deal, but confirmed that no lease has been signed. DivcoWest, which purchased 300 Howard with private equity giant Blackstone in April for $111 million, declined comment. 

The San Francisco Business Times first reported on the pending lease deal Thursday.

Once completed, Anthropic’s lease would be a major sign of confidence for the city’s burgeoning AI industry and struggling commercial real estate market, which continues to be beset with unprecedented vacancy. AI startups have been the main driver of recent leasing activity, pointing at a slow but promising turnaround. 

But most leases forged by the city’s nascent AI players, including industry front-runner OpenAI, have been short-term deals, leaving some real estate insiders wondering about the longevity of their growth trajectories.

While Anthropic already has two lease commitments on Howard Street — it has occupied 230,000 square feet for its headquarters at 500 Howard St. since 2023, or Foundry Square IV, and in September agreed to lease several floors of office space totaling about 100,000 square feet at 505 Howard St., sources have confirmed that those agreements both expire in 2028.

Anthropic has not confirmed whether it plans to consolidate its workforce at 300 Howard St., or whether it will keep all three office spaces and create a corporate campus that could rival OpenAI’s growing campus in Mission Bay, which now spans three buildings, and could soon include a fourth. 

For Anthropic, founded in 2021 by San Francisco natives and former OpenAI executives, siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei, in a small office in Jackson Square, a downtown corporate campus would be reflective of the company’s astronomical growth in recent years, fueled by investment from major tech companies including Amazon and Google.
