The questions
  1. What property traded, what product type is it, and when did the sale close (or when was it announced, if the close date isn't given)?
  2. What was the total transaction price?
  3. What was the price per gross square foot?
  4. What was the price per rentable square foot?
  5. What was the price per unit (multifamily) or per key (hotel)?
  6. What was the cap rate, and is it going-in or pro forma?
  7. Who was the seller, and who was the buyer?
  8. Was the sale distressed (foreclosure, note sale, lender-owned, receiver sale, deed in lieu)?
  9. How was it financed: loan size, lender, loan-to-value, loan-to-cost, and interest rate (fixed rate or spread over index)?
